# Seasonal conversation content audit

Reviewed: 2026-09-26

This catalog adds 13 fixed-date learning anchors with a before, active, and recent conversation for each anchor. The dates select authored practice scenarios; they do not make attendance, observance, travel, family activity, or any particular routine a requirement. All dialogue and learner-facing copy are original. Content is localized in Traditional Chinese, Japanese, and English, and uses the existing coworker, classmate, and participant roles.

## Date and content checks

The event selector is tested across every date in 2026, 2027, and 2028, including Japan Standard Time conversion and the December 31/January 1 boundary. Each event is checked at the eight-day upcoming, one-day imminent, event-day active, and fourteen-day recent boundaries against its authored association. The January 1 recent scenario is separately checked on January 2 and January 15. The 2028 fixture verifies selection under the current fixed-date rules; it is not evidence of a published official 2028 holiday roster.

The combined seasonal and evergreen definitions are checked for unique IDs, schema validity, reference closure, reachable response bindings, and completion through every authored response choice. Tests also check launched-locale coverage, all 39 phase definitions, source provenance closure, advertised learner jobs against selectable response paths, medium reciprocity and deeper long arcs, and absence of exact-day wording in broad before/recent windows. Difficulty dimensions are authored per scenario: public observations remain neutral and concrete, while reflective or personal depth is used only where the relationship and job support it.
